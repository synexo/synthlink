Pick a board, pick a protocol, press Connect, and listen.

The handshake you hear is not a recording. Data is being modulated into these tones, carried as audio, and demodulated at the other end. The carrier you hear is the data.

The modem protocols

Bell 103 at 300 baud up through V.90 at 56k. Pick one from the speed menu beside the BBS list before connecting. Each has a distinctive sound, and with the slower protocols your ear can actually detect each byte being sent over the wire.

The BBS list

Provided by the Telnet BBS Guide. {{BRAND}} handles the modem then proxies your connection to one of the many telnet BBSs still online. Browse the guide in full at telnetbbsguide.com.

Using it

  • Choose a BBS, or click the pencil to enter a host:port.
  • Click the BBS button to favorite or to dial a random board.
  • The Aa button cycles fonts.
  • Set audio control to auto (default), always on, or mute.
  • The share icon copies a link to whatever board and speed you have selected, as well as offers embedding assistance.
  • The handset icon simulates picking up an extension on the line, disrupting the carrier, just as it did in 1994.
  • Keyboard, scroll, and magnify controls are provided primarily for use on mobile.
  • On mobile, long-press the oscilloscope to hide.

On-screen Keyboard

Designed for mobile use with the needs of classic BBSs in mind.

The ⇧@# key cycles four panels: lowercase, capitals, symbols, and a numeric keypad. Long-press it, Ctrl, or Shft to hold.

The keypad's Alt key enters characters by code, as a DOS machine did: tap Alt, then three digits e.g. 219 for █ or 176 for ░.
