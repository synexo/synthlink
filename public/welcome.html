Welcome to SynthLink

A BBS terminal that dials over a real software modem.

Pick a board, pick a modem, press Connect, and listen. The handshake you hear is not a recording — your keystrokes are modulated into those tones, carried as audio, and demodulated at the other end. The carrier is the data.

Start anywhere. The default is V.34 at 33600, which is fast enough that a modern board feels responsive. The 300 bps modems are worth hearing at least once, for the sound and for how slowly the text arrives.

Everything else — the on-screen keyboard, scrollback, the extension phone, shareable links — is behind the ⓘ button in the bar above, whenever you want it.
