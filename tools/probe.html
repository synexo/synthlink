Backing store sized to cssWidth × dpr, filled with alternating 1-device-pixel columns. Clean stripes = exact mapping. Moiré, grey, or varying stripe width = the browser is resampling, and §3.1 is not satisfied.

The same measurement over a hand-drawn 1-px-stem rectangle. This is what an unresampled, unantialiased stem looks like on this device — the standard section 4 is judged against. If this shows intermediate alphas, the canvas itself is being scaled and every other number here is suspect.
